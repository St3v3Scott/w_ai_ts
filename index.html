Four strength days and one conditioning day to lose fat and keep building muscle. Tap a set when you finish it and the rest timer starts on its own.

Easy start for your first two weeks back: one fewer set, longer rests, shorter holds.

How to progress
  • Weekly rhythm: Day 1, Day 2, rest, Day 3, Day 4, Day 5, rest. Start on any day of the week.
  • Keep Easy start on for two weeks, then switch it off.
  • Hit the top of the rep range on every set, then make it harder: slower lowering (3–4 s), a pause at the hardest point, or an extra set.
  • Stop most sets 1–2 reps short of failure. The last set of push-ups can go closer.
  • 10 kg will get light for legs first. Single-leg moves and the 16 kg kettlebell buy time; adjustable dumbbells up to 20–24 kg are the best next purchase.
  • Walk 8–10k steps daily. It burns fat without eating into recovery.
Eating for fat loss while adding muscle
  • Calories: start around 2,100–2,300 a day, a mild deficit so training stays strong.
  • Protein: 150–170 g a day, spread over 3–4 meals.
  • Aim to lose 0.25–0.5 kg a week. Faster usually costs muscle.
  • Track the weekly average weight plus your waist. If weight stalls but your waist shrinks and lifts go up, it's working.
